- what it does: Active: Maokai flings a Sapling to the target location, granting sight of the area while it is in-flight. The Sapling explodes upon colliding with an enemy or when it expires, dealing magic damage to nearby enemies, capped at 300 against non-champions, and slowing them by 45% for 2 seconds. Targets can only be attached to two Saplings at a time; subsequent empowered Sapling explosions on a target will refresh the duration and only attach a Sapling if one has exploded on them already.
