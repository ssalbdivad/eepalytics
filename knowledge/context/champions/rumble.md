### Passive
- name: Junkyard Titan [wiki-p rev-3985666, fetched 2026-09-18]
- what it does: Innate: Rumble's basic ability casts generate Heat, which decays by 10 Heat per second after not using any basic ability within 4 seconds or The Equalizer within 2 seconds. Rumble's mech enters the Danger Zone when at or above 50 Heat, and becomes Overheated while at 150 Heat. Danger Zone: Rumble's mech enters the Danger Zone, empowering his basic abilities. Overheated: Rumble's mech becomes Overheated, disabling his abilities as his Heat decays back down to 0 over 4 seconds. During this time, he gains 50% – 142.54% (based on level) bonus attack speed and empowers his basic attacks to deal 5 – 44.12 (based on level) (+ 25% AP) (+ 4% of the target's maximum health) bonus magic damage on-hit. The damage based on the target's health ratio is capped at 65 – 163.32 (based on level) against monsters. [wiki-p rev-3985666, fetched 2026-09-18]
- targeting: Passive [wiki-p rev-3985666, fetched 2026-09-18]
- damage type: Magic [wiki-p rev-3985666, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: While Rumble is overheating, a Silenced icon will appear above him to signify he cannot cast abilities. This is visible to all units. [wiki-p rev-3985666, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: The attacks do not deal the bonus damage against structures. [wiki-p rev-3985666, fetched 2026-09-18]
