### R
- name: Death Mark [wiki-r rev-4050166, fetched 2026-09-18]
- cooldown: 120 / 110 / 100 [wiki-r rev-4050166, fetched 2026-09-18]
- what it does: Active: Zed becomes untargetable and, after a 0.6-second delay, he dashes to 125 units beyond the target enemy champion from his position at the end of the delay over 0.35 seconds. Afterwards, he becomes targetable again, renders the target Marked for Death for 3 seconds, and gains ghosting for the same duration. Zed also spawns a Shadow at his casting position for 9 seconds which is able to mimic Razor Shuriken and Shadow Slash regardless of range. Marked for Death: Zed stores a portion of all pre-mitigation physical damage and magic damage he and his Shadows deal to the target, detonating at the end of the duration to deal physical damage.
