### Passive
- name: Living Forge [wiki-p rev-4063954, fetched 2026-09-18]
- what it does: Innate - Living Forge: Ornn can purchase items from the shop even while outside of its range and from anywhere on the battlefield, by forging them. He is able to forge any non-consumable item, and is also presented a Forge Menu to quickly forge his recommended items. Once Ornn attempts to purchase an item, he starts forging it, causing him to channel for 4 seconds. Upon completion of the channel, he builds the item and pays its gold cost. The channel is interrupted if Ornn takes damage from enemy champions or turrets. Living Forge is disabled for 4 seconds after starting the channel. Innate - Master Craftsman: Ornn uses his craftsmanship in forgery to upgrade most Legendary items that he and his teammates own into Masterwork items, increasing the gold value of all upgradeable stats on the item by exactly 1000, shared equally among them. When Ornn reaches level 13, the first eligible Legendary item in his inventory is upgraded automatically. For each level after 13 and before 18 (levels 14–17, total of 4, once per ally per game), Ornn can upgrade the first eligible Legendary item in an allied champion's inventory into its Masterwork variant. He can do so by selecting the ally while within 600 range, and forges the item's upgrade by performing Living Forge's channel. Additionally, Ornn increases his bonus armor, bonus magic resistance and bonus health by 10% from all sources, further increased by 4% each time he upgrades a Legendary item into a Masterwork item, for a total increase of 30% to each stat after the maximum of 5 upgrades. Innate - Temper: Ornn applies Brittle to enemies hit by Bellows Breath and Call of the Forge God. His basic attacks on-hit against enemies afflicted with Brittle are empowered to knock them back a short distance over 0.4 seconds. Ornn's and allies' immobilizing effects or silences against Brittle enemies will consume the debuff to deal bonus magic damage equal to 9% – 17.94% (based on Ornn's level) of the target's maximum health, capped at 200 – 500 (based on Bellows Breath's rank) against monsters, and cause the crowd control to last 30% longer (if applicable). Ornn will prioritize upgrading the first Legendary item eligible for a Masterwork upgrade based on its position in the inventory, detecting each slot until an eligible item is found.
