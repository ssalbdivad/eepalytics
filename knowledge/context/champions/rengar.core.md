### Passive
- name: Unseen Predator [wiki-p rev-4059878, fetched 2026-09-18]
- what it does: Innate: Upon casting a basic ability or using Savagery's first attack, Rengar generates a stack of Ferocity for 1 second, stacking up 4 times but not refreshing on subsequent triggers (unexpected). At maximum stacks, his next basic ability consumes them all to become empowered with an additional effect and grant him an additional cast along with 30% / 40% / 50% (based on level) bonus movement speed for 1.5 seconds. Generated Ferocity is prevented from expiring for 10 seconds after dealing or taking damage, excluding damage dealt by damage over time or proc effects. Innate: While in brush, Rengar gains Unseen Predator, which lingers for 0.35 seconds upon exiting brush. Unseen Predator is lost immediately if Rengar exits the brush using Flash or Hexflash. Unseen Predator: Rengar's next basic attack on-attack generates 1 Ferocity if none of his current Ferocity was generated this way. The attack also has massively increased range and causes him to leap to the target's location if they are beyond his normal basic attack range, attacking the target upon arrival and landing 50 units in front of them. Rengar can cast any of his abilities during the dash. The start of the leap is the end of Rengar's attack windup, putting the attack timer on cooldown, triggering on-attack effects and additionally refreshing the Ferocity duration. Innate - Bonetooth Necklace: Scoring a takedown against an enemy champion within 3 seconds of damaging them grants Rengar a Trophy, once per unique champion and up to 5. An additional Trophy is made available through the event The Hunt is On! with the Head of Kha'Zix. Trophies grant bonus attack damage equal to 1% – 36% (based on Trophies) bonus AD.
