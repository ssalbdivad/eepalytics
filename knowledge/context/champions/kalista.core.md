### Passive
- name: Martial Poise [wiki-p rev-4024157, fetched 2026-09-18]
- what it does: Innate: When Kalista inputs a movement command during her basic attack windup or shortly after it completes, or while in cast time of Pierce, she will dash in that direction. Kalista cannot dash while immobilized or grounded, and she will be knocked down by any immobilizing or polymorphing crowd control during the dash. Martial Poise's dash range and speed details: - Dash range of basic attacks is modified by the tier of Kalista's Boots and the angle of the dash. - At a 0° angle towards the target, the minimum dash range is 140 / 153 / 166 / 175 (based on Boots Tier) units. - At a 90° angle, the maximum dash range is 240 / 263 / 280 / 300 (based on Boots Tier) units. - Dash range of Pierce is 300 units when dashing anywhere in the direction of the point of cast. - If the dash is inputted to a location that is away from Pierce's point of cast, the dash range is reduced based on the angle, with a minimum distance of 150 units if the inputted location is directly opposite. - Dash speed is increased based on the tier of Kalista's Boots, and is affected by her bonus attack speed as well as movement speed modifiers she is under the effect of (including slows). Innate - Oathsworn Bond: Kalista begins the game with an exclusive Black Spear that she can activate on an allied champion to deem them Oathsworn, forming a tether together. While she and the ally bound as the Oathsworn are within tether range of each other, they can both interact with Soul-Mark and Fate's Call. Kalista can purchase the Black Spear from the shop at no cost to obtain it again and, before 3 minutes of game time, may activate the item again on a different target to designate a new Oathsworn. See Black Spear for more details. [wiki-p rev-4024157, fetched 2026-09-18]
- targeting: Direction [wiki-p rev-4024157, fetched 2026-09-18]

### E
- what it does: Passive: Kalista's basic attacks on-hit and Pierce apply a stack of Rend to enemies for 4 seconds, refreshing on subsequent hits and stacking up to 254 times per enemy. Active: Kalista rips out the lodged spears from all nearby enemies afflicted with Rend stacks, consuming the stacks on each target to deal physical damage and slow them for 2 seconds. For each stack beyond the first, the target is dealt additional physical damage. Rend deals 50% damage against epic monsters. Rend's cast also empowers the in-flight missiles of Kalista's basic attacks and Pierce to apply Rend's effects to their targets immediately upon hitting them. If Rend kills at least one target, its cooldown is reset and Kalista restores mana. A nearby enemy with a Rend stack is required to cast this ability. [wiki-e rev-4004071, fetched 2026-09-18]

### R
- name: Fate's Call [wiki-r rev-4015001, fetched 2026-09-18]
- cooldown: 160 / 140 / 120 [wiki-r rev-4015001, fetched 2026-09-18]
- cost: 100 [wiki-r rev-4015001, fetched 2026-09-18]
- what it does: Active: Kalista invokes her tether to retrieve and hold her Oathsworn for 4 seconds. Kalista cleanses the Oathsworn from all crowd control and renders them invulnerable and untargetable for the duration, and pulls them to her over 1 second. While held, the Oathsworn is vanished and may select a target location to dash to with displacement immunity, ending Fate's Call's invocation and reappearing at the location. If the invocation ends without the Oathsworn selecting a target location, they will automatically do so at maximum range from Kalista's facing direction. The Oathsworn's dash stops upon colliding with an enemy champion. Upon the dash ending by collision or arriving to the targeted location, the Oathsworn knocks back all nearby enemies and keeps them airborne for a set duration, while simultaneously landing to their base attack range from the closest target hit. The Oathsworn must be within tether range to cast this ability, and is also silenced and unable to perform movement or attack commands while Fate's Call is in effect.
