Martial Poise's dash range and speed details: - Dash range of basic attacks is modified by the tier of Kalista's Boots and the angle of the dash. - At a 0° angle towards the target, the minimum dash range is 140 / 153 / 166 / 175 (based on Boots Tier) units. - At a 90° angle, the maximum dash range is 240 / 263 / 280 / 300 (based on Boots Tier) units. - Dash range of Pierce is 300 units when dashing anywhere in the direction of the point of cast. - If the dash is inputted to a location that is away from Pierce's point of cast, the dash range is reduced based on the angle, with a minimum distance of 150 units if the inputted location is directly opposite. - Dash speed is increased based on the tier of Kalista's Boots, and is affected by her bonus attack speed as well as movement speed modifiers she is under the effect of (including slows). Innate - Oathsworn Bond: Kalista begins the game with an exclusive Black Spear that she can activate on an allied champion to deem them Oathsworn, forming a tether together. While she and the ally bound as the Oathsworn are within tether range of each other, they can both interact with Soul-Mark and Fate's Call. Kalista can purchase the Black Spear from the shop at no cost to obtain it again and, before 3 minutes of game time, may activate the item again on a different target to designate a new Oathsworn.
