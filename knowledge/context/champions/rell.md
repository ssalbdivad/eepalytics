### Passive
- name: Break the Mold [wiki-p rev-4050368, fetched 2026-09-18]
- what it does: Innate: Rell's basic attacks deal bonus magic damage on-hit equal to the sum of 5% of her total armor and 5% of her total magic resistance. Innate: Rell's basic attacks on-hit and abilities against non- minions apply a stack of Break The Mold for 5 seconds, refreshing on subsequent hits and stacking up to 5 times. Each stack reduces the target's armor and magic resistance by 3% for a maximum of 15% reduction. Rell gains bonus armor and bonus magic resistance equal to the sum of resistances reduced from all afflicted enemies. Break the Mold will reduce the target's armor and magic resistance by a minimum of 1.5 – 3.18 (based on level). [wiki-p rev-4050368, fetched 2026-09-18]
- targeting: Passive [wiki-p rev-4050368, fetched 2026-09-18]
- damage type: magic [wiki-p rev-4050368, fetched 2026-09-18]
- spell shield: false [wiki-p rev-4050368, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: The attacks do not affect structures. [wiki-p rev-4050368, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: The first stack of Break the Mold that is applied to a target will snapshot the targets current armor and magic resistance. All subsequent stacks will reduce the targets resistances based on the snapshotted amount, even if the target's total resistances change. This snapshot lasts until the debuff expires. [wiki-p rev-4050368, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: For example, applying a stack of Break the Mold to a champion with 100 armor and magic resistance will reduce their resistance by 3 and grant Rell the lost stats. If their resistances are then boosted to 500 before another stack is applied, the subsequent stack will only reduce their resistance by a further 3 despite 3% of 500 being 15. [wiki-p rev-4050368, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: Another example, when a target has temporarily gained increased resistances before Rell applies the first stack of Break the Mold then the snapshot of the target's stats will be of the increased amount. This can result in targets losing significantly more resistances after the duration of the increase ends, for as long as the stacks don't fall off. [wiki-p rev-4050368, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: If a target has 100 base resistances, and they are increased to 500 temporarily before Break the Mold is applied, then the target will lose a total of 15 resistances, up to a total of 75 resistances at 5 stacks, and when the target's resistance buff ends they would only have 25 remaining. [wiki-p rev-4050368, fetched 2026-09-18]
