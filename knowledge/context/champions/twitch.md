The bolts deal 100% – 60% (based on enemies hit) of the triggering attack's damage, apply on-hit effects, and can critically strike for (200% + 30%) damage. [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- damage by rank: Bonus Attack Damage: 30 / 45 / 60 [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]

- cast time: none [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- width: 120 [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- missile speed: 5000 [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- targeting: Auto [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- damage type: Physical [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: The extra distance that the bolts travel scales with bonus attack range. [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: Spray and Pray allows Twitch to outrange turrets by 50 units, allowing him to hit them without them returning fire. [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: Twitch targets his line attack at his target's location at the start of his attack windup. [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: The bolts reach targets at a delay, composed of Twitch's windup time and distance the bolt has to travel at finite speed. Even the primary target can dodge the attack if they are moving quickly enough. [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: On-attack effects such as Help, Pix! and Runaan's Hurricane's Wind's Fury will be triggered as usual from the completion of Twitch's basic attack windup. They will obey their standard target-acquisition rules in spite of Spray and Pray's special basic attack behavior. [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: The bolt projectiles will naturally pass through terrain and enemy structures (the latter will be damaged by bolts even if they are not directly targeted). [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: If Twitch is blinded before winding up the attack, the hits will miss against all targets. [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: Runaan's Hurricane's Wind's Fury interacts with Spray and Pray's bonus attack range but not with the modified missile effect (the secondary bolts will not have pass-through effects). [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: Whenever the bolts penetrate a target, a small elongated cloud appears at the location, which's VFX and SFX can be seen and heard inside the Fog of War. [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: The bolts' travel distance scales with the full value of Twitch's attack range increases such as Rapid Firecannon, but not with size increases (which only increase his effective attack range, thereby the target range). [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
- edge case: (Outdated as of V10.13, now can hit everything with edge range, except for turrets) The center of a unit must be within the maximum travel distance of the bolt missile, and in front of the spawn location (Twitch's center) for the bolt to be able to hit them. - edge case: Other than this condition, the bolt missile has to only touch (pass within its half width of 60 units) the edge of the unit's radius. [wiki-r rev-4063223, fetched 2026-09-18]
