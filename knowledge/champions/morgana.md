# Morgana — the counterpart, and how Lillia plays against her

Read when Morgana is the **enemy jungler**. She is not a traditional jungler and she is a real pick,
so her presence in the jungle is itself information: she is there to clear safely with a
ground-effect ability and to turn one long-range root into a kill.

## The kit, as it affects Lillia

Morgana is "she" throughout this file; the player, on Lillia, is "he".

**Passive — Soul Siphon.** She heals off damage dealt to champions, large minions and medium and
larger monsters. Her clear costs her almost nothing in health, and every camp tops her back up. "She
just cleared, she must be low" is false about Morgana.

**Q — Dark Binding.** A long-range skillshot that **roots**. It is the entire champion. A landed Q
against a jungler whose escape is movement speed is a dead jungler; a missed Q is a Morgana with
nothing for a long cooldown. Everything else in her kit is a way of making the root pay.

**W — Tormented Shadow.** She ignites an area of ground that burns over several seconds and does
**more damage the lower the target's health is**. This is her clear engine and her camp damage, and
its cooldown is refunded whenever her passive heals her — so on camps it comes back quickly. In a
fight it is the reason a root is fatal: she puts the pool under a rooted target and it does not have
to be aimed again.

**E — Black Shield.** A magic shield on an ally or herself that grants **crowd control immunity for
as long as it holds** — and the crowd control is negated *before* any magic damage is absorbed, so
breaking the shield in the same instant does not let the disable through. The matchup-defining
ability, and the reason she is picked into crowd control.

**R — Soul Shackles.** She chains nearby enemy champions, slows them, and after a delay **stuns
everyone who did not get far enough away**. It is an area stun on a short leash, and her only way to
start a fight against someone who is already running.

I have not written her cooldowns, root duration, shield strength or the chain-break distance.

## Reading her clear

- **Safe and sustained rather than fast.** She takes camps with the burn pool and heals through
them, but her early clear speed is unremarkable and she is **very mana-hungry**.
- **A Morgana even on CS is doing well for the champion.** Ahead on CS against her is an achievable
and expected state for a Lillia running full routes.
- **Her mana is the honest early weakness.** A Morgana without blue runs out, and a Morgana out of
mana can neither clear nor threaten a root.
- **A Morgana behind on CS has been sitting in a lane brush** waiting for the one root that pays
for the whole detour. That is what this pick does with its time.

## Her ganks, and what makes them fail

She walks to a lane, hides where the angle is bad, and throws the root from a long way off. If it
lands, the lane usually dies; if it misses, nothing happens at all and she has wasted a minute.

They fail on **the width of the skillshot and the angle** — it is a single-target line and it is
stopped by anything in front of the target, minions included. They fail on mana. And they fail on
vision, because her approach is a walk with no dash in it.

## What her items tell you

She is an ability-power champion in every build, and the tell is what shape.

- **Burn and health-scaling ability power** is the damage plan: her pool does most of the work and
a root plus the pool is a kill on a squishy.
- **Ability haste and defensive items** is the support-shaped plan: she is there to root and shield
for someone else, and she will be near her carries rather than in the enemy jungle.
- **A completed first item on her** roughly doubles what a landed root costs. Before it, being
rooted is survivable; after it, mostly it is not.

## What her presence means for drakes and grubs

- She is a mediocre solo pit fighter — the burn pool is fine damage on an objective and she has no
way to burst one down or to win a smite contest on damage.
- **Her objective play is the root at the entrance.** She does not contest the pit, she catches
whoever walks to it. The dangerous ground is the river corridor, not the pit.
- Her ultimate turns a stacked objective fight into a multi-person stun, which is the same fight
shape a Lillia ultimate wants. Whichever of them lands theirs first usually wins that fight.

## HOW LILLIA PLAYS THE MORGANA MATCHUP

**Who wins the early duel: whoever lands their skillshot, and Morgana's is far more punishing.**
This is not a damage matchup, it is a single-button matchup. Rooted, he has no movement speed and
therefore no kit; unrooted, he kites a champion with no dash for as long as he likes and wins
comfortably. Treat every duel with Morgana as a coin flip on one ability, and decline coin flips.

**Who wins the race: Lillia.** His clear accelerates, Morgana's is mana-bound and merely safe. This
is a matchup where the farm lead is available and should be taken rather than gambled on a fight.

**The thing that must be said about her shield: Black Shield blocks the sleep.** A Lilting Lullaby
cast on a shielded target does nothing to that target, and Morgana can put it on herself or on the
person he most wanted asleep. The correct habit is to **make the shield be spent first** — it has a
long cooldown and it breaks on damage — rather than to lead with the ult into it.

**When he may contest a crab or an objective:**

- In open ground where the root can be dodged, approaching at an angle rather than in a straight line at her.
- When Morgana is visibly low on mana, or when the Q has just been used and missed.
- When his team is present and the ult can be held for the moment Morgana commits her own.

**When he absolutely may not contest:**

- In the river corridor, where the root is a straight line he is already walking down.
- At even health with Black Shield up on the target he plans to sleep.
- At low mana, because kiting a Morgana requires the abilities that do the kiting.
- Off a gank angle that walks him toward an unseen brush — the root does not need him to be low, it only needs him to be still.

**The trap specific to this matchup:** thinking of Morgana as a weak jungler because her clear is
slow and her ganks are unreliable. All of that is true and none of it matters at the moment the root
lands. The whole matchup is one ability, and the answer to it is spacing and angles, not health
bars.
