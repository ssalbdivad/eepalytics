Bel'Veth is a **scaling skirmisher** built around stacking. She wants repeated fights and
objective takes that feed her, and she gets stronger the longer the game runs and the more she
has eaten. This makes her the opposite of a champion you coach toward safety.

**Her early clear is not fast and her early duel is not free.** The first few minutes are the
weakest part of her game; forcing an early invade or a coin-flip fight before she has stacks is
the most common way the game is lost outright.

**Her power comes from the objective loop, not from lanes.** She converts objective control into
permanent power, so pathing that keeps her near contestable objectives compounds in a way that
ganking a losing lane does not.

**She wants long fights in open space** — dashes, resets, and sustained damage. A short fight in
a corridor is her worst case.
