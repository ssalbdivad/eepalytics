Bel'Veth is a **scaling skirmisher** built around stacking. She wants repeated fights and
objective takes that feed her, and she gets stronger the longer the game runs and the more she
has eaten. This makes her the opposite of a champion you coach toward safety.

**Her early clear is not fast and her early duel is not free.** The first few minutes are the
weakest part of her game; forcing an early invade or a coin-flip fight before she has stacks is
the most common way the game is lost outright.

**Her power comes from the objective loop, not from lanes.** She converts objective control into
permanent power, so pathing that keeps her near contestable objectives compounds in a way that
ganking a losing lane does not.

**She wants long fights in open space** — dashes, resets, and sustained damage. A short fight in
a corridor is her worst case.

## Live coaching

One clause per situation, appended to a number the client proved. **No digits** — refused at load
time. Several clauses per key is intentional; the app rotates through them on the game clock.

race-behind: your power comes from stacking, so the route that finishes is worth more than the gank that might not
race-behind: you are the one who gets paid for a long game, so take the camps and stop trading tempo for a coin flip

race-ahead: keep eating, the lead compounds on you in a way it does not on him
race-ahead: turn it into an objective, because that is the kind of power you keep after a death timer

restraint: you have very little before you have stacks, so an even fight now is the worst version of this champion
restraint: decline it and go stack, this is the one matchup shape that gets better every minute you refuse it

fight-lost: you want the long fight in open space where the dashes and the resets pay, not a short one in a corridor
fight-lost: you arrive to a fight rather than starting one, so the entry has to be after someone else is already committed

fight-absent: you convert objectives into permanent power in a way that ganking a losing lane never does, so go take one
fight-absent: the fight was decided before you got there, the objective on the other side of the map was not

death: your escape is the dash chain and it needs somewhere to go, so do not walk into a place with one exit
death: your stacks die with you, which makes a greedy fight cost you more than it costs anyone else on the map

build: you get stronger with the game and he does not, so read that item as a reason to take the fight later
build: that is his spike arriving early, so stop contesting on his terms and go stack somewhere he is not

farm-behind: every camp is stacks as well as gold, so a route left unfinished costs you twice
farm-behind: your early clear is not fast, and the fix for that is finishing it rather than cutting it short
