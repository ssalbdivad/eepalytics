# Briar — the counterpart, and how Lillia plays against her

This file exists to be read when Briar is the **enemy jungler**. Everything below is about what
she is doing while he is clearing, and what it means for him.

## What she is

A frenzied, self-healing skirmisher with one of the fastest and healthiest clears in the game and
no way out of a fight she starts. She is not a scaling jungler and she is not a pathing jungler —
she is a jungler who wants to be in melee range of someone right now, and who is dramatically
better at that than Lillia is.

## The kit, as it affects him

**Passive — Crimson Curse.** Her damage applies a bleed, and she **heals from that bleed**. In
exchange she has effectively no out-of-combat health regeneration. This is the single most
important fact about her: **she does not leave camps low.** She heals herself back up by killing
things, so "she just cleared her whole topside, she must be hurt" is false about Briar in a way
it is true about most junglers. Planning an invade around her being weak after a clear is the
classic way to hand her a kill.

**Q — Head Rush.** A dash to a target with damage and hard CC on arrival. This is her gap-closer
and her gank opener. It is also, mostly, her only one before 6.

**W — Blood Frenzy / Snack Attack.** She removes her own restraints: she gains attack speed and
lifesteal and becomes **frenzied**, automatically attacking whatever is nearest her. Recast bites
the target for a chunk of health and heals her. The frenzy is her clear speed and her sustain,
and it is also her one exploitable flaw — **while frenzied she attacks the nearest thing, not the
thing she meant to attack.** A minion, a jungle monster or a pet standing between her and her
target genuinely redirects her.

**E — Chilling Scream.** A charged scream that damages and **knocks enemies back — into terrain
for a stun.** She wants a wall behind her target. It is also the ability she uses to snap herself
out of frenzy. This is why a fight against Briar in a river corridor or a jungle entrance is a
different fight from the same one in open ground.

**R — Certain Death.** A very long-range leap onto a champion, fearing enemies near the landing
and putting her into frenzy on that target with bonus stats. It is an engage from off-screen and
it is the reason "she is not on my screen" is not the same as "she cannot reach me" after 6.

I have not written her cooldowns, her healing percentages, her leap range, or the exact stun
duration on the knockback — those move with patches and none of them are worth a wrong number.

## Reading her clear

- **She clears fast and finishes healthy.** Expect her CS to keep pace with a good Lillia clear.
  If her CS is level with his, that is her normal state and not a sign he is behind.
- **If she is meaningfully ahead on CS**, she has been full-clearing and is on a route, not
  hovering a lane — which is the safest time for him to take a crab or a far camp, and the
  worst time to path toward a lane hoping to catch her.
- **If she is meaningfully behind on CS at level 5 or later**, she has been ganking or fighting.
  That is more dangerous, not less: a Briar who is behind on farm and even on kills has been
  spending her time near lanes, and the next place she appears is a lane.
- **A completed first item on her while he has none** is the moment to stop contesting anything
  neutral in melee range. Her item spikes convert directly into duel-winning.

## Her ganks, and what makes them fail

She ganks with Q, or with R from a long way off. They fail for two reasons and only two: **she is
seen coming**, because she has no stealth and no reposition that hides her approach, and **the
frenzy picks the wrong target** — she gets stuck auto-attacking a minion wave or a pet while the
target walks away. Neither of those is something he can rely on, but both are things a lane that
has vision can play around.

Once she has committed — Q in, or R in — **she has no exit**. A Briar dive that does not kill is
usually a dead Briar. That is the shape of every fight she loses.

## HOW LILLIA PLAYS THE BRIAR MATCHUP

This is the section that matters.

**Who wins the early duel: Briar, clearly, and it is not close.** She out-sustains, out-damages
and out-CCs Lillia at every point before Lillia's first completed item, and her frenzy healing
means a long fight is *her* format too, not just Lillia's. He must not take an even-health 1v1
with Briar in the first ten minutes. Not at a crab, not at a camp, not to defend a camp. The
camp is worth less than the death.

**Who wins the race: Lillia, if she keeps moving.** Both clears are fast; Briar's is fast and
healthy, Lillia's is fast and getting faster. The difference is that Briar's speed does not carry
between camps and Lillia's does. So the winning posture in this matchup is **trade camps, never
trade blows.** If Briar takes his raptors, the answer is to be in her red-side jungle taking hers,
not to walk back and contest.

**When he may contest a crab:**

- When he knows where Briar is and it is not here — Swirlseed rolled down the river or the
  entrance is the way to know that, and it takes two seconds.
- When he is meaningfully ahead in level or health and there is **open ground behind him**, not a
  wall for her E to knock him into.
- When he has his ult, mana for it, and the dust can go on her before he casts — a drowsy Briar
  is one of the few ways Lillia wins a fight she did not want.

**When he absolutely may not contest a crab:**

- At even health before his first item. Ever.
- At low mana. He cannot fight and he cannot leave.
- In the river corridor with a wall behind him, which is most of the river.
- When he cannot say where she is. Against a champion with a long-range leap engage, "she is
  probably topside" is not information.

**The one trap specific to this matchup:** the instinct to punish her after a clear. She heals
through her clear, so the health bar he is imagining is not the health bar she has. If he wants
to punish Briar, the moment is **after a failed gank or a committed R**, not after a camp.
