## Live coaching

Role advice, and the fallback for every champion. These clauses fill any situation the champion's
own file leaves bare, so they have to be things that are true of *the jungle*, never of a
champion. Kept deliberately plain: a generic clause that fits any jungler is still better than a
specific one that was invented. **No digits** — refused at load time.

Several clauses per key is intentional; the app rotates through them on the game clock.

race-behind: counter-jungling pays when it costs him tempo and costs you when you are the one behind, so take your own
race-behind: take the camps between you and him before you take the fight, you win that race more reliably than the duel

race-ahead: keep the route full and spend the lead on an objective rather than on a duel
race-ahead: the lead is tempo, so use it to be somewhere before he can be

restraint: decline it, a coin flip you walk away from costs a camp and one you lose costs the objective
restraint: take your own camps instead and be full health when that timer comes up

fight-lost: the second fight of a lost fight is where a survivable game stops being one, so reset
fight-lost: come back even rather than forcing the rematch on their timer

fight-absent: that was decided before you could reach it, the far side of the map was not
fight-absent: be on the map before the next one starts rather than after it

death: the useful thing now is what will be true when you come back, so pick the timer you are walking to
death: check the entry with something that costs you nothing before you cross next time

build: assume the duel just got worse and take the fight you already had the numbers for
build: that is a real spike on him, so stop planning the even fight until you have reset

farm-behind: a full clear beats a gank that is not ready, so run the next route to the end
farm-behind: dead time is the largest hidden cost in this role and nothing on your screen counts it
