## Live coaching

Role advice — the fallback for every champion, and the fill for any kind a champion file
leaves bare. Every clause here has to be true of *the jungle*, never of a champion.
Half-sentences, said out loud after a number the client has already proved. **No digits** —
a figure here would be a claim about the game, and every figure he hears must come from the
client. Anything with a number in it is refused at load time, deliberately.

Several clauses per key is intentional: a fixed clause per kind means the identical half-sentence
every time that kind fires, and sameness is what made him stop listening. Each one has to end on
something he can DO in the next thirty seconds.

race-behind: take the camps that are up before you take the fight, you win that race more reliably
race-behind: answer farm with farm, and gank only the lane that can turn it into a plate
race-behind: cross for his camps when yours are dead, not instead of yours
race-behind: ward the entrance before you cross, rather than assuming he is elsewhere

race-ahead: spend the lead on an objective rather than a duel, the objective is the part you keep
race-ahead: you are ahead on tempo, so be at the next timer before he can be
race-ahead: keep the route full and take his camps while yours come back

restraint: decline it, a coin flip you walk away from costs a camp and one you lose costs the pit
restraint: take your own camps instead and be full health when that timer comes up
restraint: the level comes back off a clear, so buy the level before you buy the fight

fight-lost: reset and come back even rather than forcing the rematch on their timer
fight-lost: the second fight after a lost one is where a game stops being survivable, so farm
fight-lost: clear your own half while their cooldowns run, then look at the map again

fight-absent: that one was already decided, the objective across the map is not
fight-absent: start walking to the next one now rather than after the ping
fight-absent: if you cannot be in it, make it cost them, take the camps they walked away from

death: check the entry with a ward before you cross next time, not once you are already inside.
death: when you respawn, clear your own side first and let their timers run down.
death: walk back toward the next objective timer instead of the fight you just lost.
death: the fight went ahead without you either way, so buy for the next one, not the last one.

build: assume the duel just got worse and take the fight you already had the numbers for
build: that is a real spike on him, so avoid the even fight until you have reset too
build: he paid for that, so make him spend it somewhere that is not the pit you want

farm-behind: run the next route to the end, a gank that is not ready is not one
farm-behind: take the camps on the way to where you were going, not a lap back
farm-behind: soak the wave crashing into your own turret while camps come back
