# Karthus — the counterpart, and how Lillia plays against him

Read when Karthus is the **enemy jungler**. Karthus jungle is a long-standing real pick rather than
a novelty: his toggled aura clears camps quickly and he wants exactly the uninterrupted farm the
jungle gives.

## What he is

An immobile scaling mage with a fast area clear, no escape of any kind, a death that does not stop
him, and an ultimate that reaches every enemy on the map at once. He is not a jungler who fights for
the map. He is a jungler who farms until his ultimate is the reason the game ends.

## The kit, as it affects her

**Passive — Death Defied.** When he dies he keeps casting for several seconds as a spirit. Killing
Karthus does not end his participation in the fight, and a Karthus who dies at a drake still casts
his ultimate afterwards. **"We killed Karthus" is not the same sentence as "Karthus stopped."**

**Q — Lay Waste.** A small delayed blast at a chosen point, on a very short cooldown, that does
**much more damage when it hits only one target**. So it is weak spam in a camp and heavy
single-target damage against a lone champion. Against her, every one of them is dodgeable and every
one she fails to dodge is real damage.

**W — Wall of Pain.** A wall of ground that **slows anyone who passes through it and reduces their
magic resistance**. This is the only crowd control he has and it is the one that matters most
against Lillia, because it is aimed at the exact thing her kit is: the ability to leave. He drops it
between her and the exit, not on top of her.

**E — Defile.** A toggled aura of damage around him, paid for in mana per second, refunded in mana
whenever he gets a kill on anything. It is his clear, it is most of his teamfight damage, and it is
the reason his mana bar collapses if he is careless.

**R — Requiem.** After a channel of a few seconds, **damage to every enemy champion on the map
regardless of distance**. It executes low targets across the whole game, and it is why being at low
health anywhere is not safe while Karthus is alive. Or dead. His passive means he can cast it while
dying.

I have not written cooldowns, mana costs, the spirit-form duration, wall duration or damage values.

## Reading his clear

- **Quick and even, once Defile is online**, because the aura kills a whole camp at once. His clear
  is one of the more consistent in this directory.
- **Mana is his real constraint.** Defile drains continuously, so a Karthus who has cleared without
  blue is on a short leash and cannot do anything that requires the toggle.
- **Expect his CS to be high, and do not treat that as the headline.** Farm is what this champion is
  for. The number that matters against Karthus is his **level and item count**, because his
  ultimate's damage and his clear both scale with them.
- **A Karthus behind on CS is a Karthus failing at the only thing he was picked to do.** That is the
  version of the matchup she is winning, and it comes from taking his camps, not from duelling him.

## His ganks, and what makes them fail

He has no dash, no stealth and no hard crowd control, so he does not gank so much as **walk into a
lane that is already low** and turn on the aura behind a wall of slow.

They fail against anything with a summoner spell or a dash, and they fail whenever the lane simply
walks out before Defile has ticked enough. His threat to a healthy lane before 6 is close to nil.

After 6 he does not need to be there at all. That is the point of the champion.

## What his items tell you

Ability power and mana, in every build.

- **Each completed item raises the floor of his ultimate**, which is the one number in the game that
  reaches her wherever she is standing. A two-item Karthus turns "I am at a third health, I will
  walk home" into a death.
- **Mana-shaped items early** mean his clear is about to stop being constrained, which usually shows
  up as a sudden jump in his CS rate.
- He builds no mobility and no escape at any point. Nothing in his feed will ever make him harder to
  catch — only harder to survive.

## What his presence means for drakes and grubs

- He is a **good objective-damage champion and a terrible pit fighter**: the aura chews through a
  drake, and he has no way to win a contested smite or to survive being jumped.
- The genuine danger at an objective is the aftermath. He can die in the pit and still finish
  everyone with the ultimate, so an objective fight "won" against Karthus can end with three of her
  team dead anyway.
- **His presence in a pit is a reason to fight him, not to avoid him** — he is the one counterpart
  in this directory who is simply weaker than her in a straight jungle skirmish at most points of
  the game.

## HOW LILLIA PLAYS THE KARTHUS MATCHUP

**Who wins the early duel: Lillia, in the open, and it is one of the few duels she should actually
want.** He cannot dash, he cannot escape, and his single-target damage requires her to stand in the
same spot she was in a moment ago. She has a slow, a long-range revealing seed, and movement speed
that grows. The one caveat is that his aura plus his wall is real damage if she chooses to fight
inside it rather than kiting around it.

**Who wins the race: roughly even, and the race is not where this game is decided.** Both clears are
fast. Farming evenly with Karthus is losing slowly, because his farm becomes a global ultimate and
hers becomes a jungler. The lever is to take his camps and his tempo so that he is not merely even —
**counter-farming Karthus is unusually safe and unusually valuable**, because he cannot punish an
invade and he cannot catch her leaving.

**When she may contest a crab or an objective:**

- Almost always, in the open, when she can see him — she beats him to it and she beats him in it.
- Deep in his own jungle, when she knows where he is. He has no answer to a stolen camp.
- At any point where his mana is visibly gone, because a Karthus without Defile has almost no damage.

**When she absolutely may not contest:**

- Inside his Wall of Pain, which is placed to take away exactly the movement speed her kit is built
  around. Fight on the side of the wall she can leave from.
- **At low health, anywhere on the map, while his ultimate is available.** This is not about
  contesting a crab; it is about the walk home afterwards. The correct habit against Karthus is to
  back one camp early rather than to path home at a sliver.
- In a drawn-out fight at low health next to three teammates who are also low — that is the shape
  Requiem is designed to end.

**The trap specific to this matchup:** treating the jungle matchup as the game. She beats Karthus
head-to-head, comfortably, all game. It does not matter if he farms undisturbed to three items,
because his win condition never involved meeting her. **Every advantage she wins against Karthus has
to be spent on objectives and on shortening the game**, not banked.
