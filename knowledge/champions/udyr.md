# Udyr — the counterpart, and how Lillia plays against him

Read when Udyr is the **enemy jungler**.

## What he is

The purest early-game jungler in this directory: an extremely fast clear, an extremely strong
duel, a stun that comes attached to a dash, and no ranged threat of any kind. He has four basic
abilities and no conventional ultimate — he **swaps stances**, and recasting a stance "Awakens" it
for a stronger version.

**He is the counterpart she must never fight, and the one she can most reliably out-pace on the
map.**

## The kit, as it affects her

**Passive — Bridge Between.** He has four stances instead of three basics and an ultimate, and
after using an ability his next two attacks gain attack speed. Recasting a stance renews it with
**Awakened** bonuses. The structure matters for one practical reason: **he always has every tool
available**, so "he used his escape" or "his engage is down" is rarely a true sentence about Udyr.

**Q — Wilding Claw.** Attack speed and bonus on-hit damage, including damage scaling on the
target's maximum health. This is his clear speed and most of his duel damage.

**W — Iron Mantle.** A shield, with life steal and healing on his next attacks; Awakened it heals
more over time. This is his sustain and it is why his clear costs him so little and why an even
duel against him goes badly even when the damage is close.

**E — Blazing Stampede.** Move speed, **and his attacks dash him to the target and stun them**.
This is the ganking and the chasing button, and the stun is per-target on a short internal
cooldown, so it happens more than once. Awakened it also makes him **immune to immobilising and
disabling effects** and faster still.

That last clause is the one that matters most to her: **an Awakened Stampede is unstoppable, and a
sleep is a disable.** If he has committed to it, the ultimate does not save her.

**R — Wingborne Storm.** A persistent storm around him that damages and **slows** everyone nearby,
with extra damage on his next attacks; Awakened it follows his last target and adds max-health
damage. In a chase, the slow does to her exactly what she is trying to do to him.

I have not written cooldowns, stance timers, shield or heal values, stun duration or the internal
cooldown on the stun.

## Reading his clear

- **One of the fastest and healthiest clears in the game**, with a shield and life steal built in.
  He is full-clearing from the first camp and he is not soft afterwards.
- **Expect him to be ahead on CS early, and do not read that as him ignoring the map.** That is
  simply what his clear does. His CS being high is not evidence he is farming rather than coming.
- **A Udyr behind on CS is a Udyr who has been fighting, and he usually wins those.** Behind on CS
  and ahead on kills is the version to be most careful of.
- **His mana is the one real constraint.** All four stances cost mana and he spends them constantly;
  a Udyr who has skipped blue is on a much shorter leash than his kit suggests.

## His ganks, and what makes them fail

He runs at the lane in Stampede, fast, and the stun happens the moment he lands an attack — there is
no skillshot and nothing to dodge. Then a second stun a moment later. Once he is next to someone,
the gank has already worked.

They fail on **vision and distance only.** A ward that sees the approach is a gank denied; everything
else about it is reliable. Post-Awakened Stampede, even crowd control on the approach does not
deny it.

## What his items tell you

He builds as a bruiser or a tank with on-hit damage attached, and the exact split moves with patches.

- **Health and resistances early** mean he is playing to be unkillable in skirmishes and around
  objectives — the version that is hardest for a champion with no burst to ever finish.
- **On-hit and attack speed** mean he intends to duel and to take objectives quickly, and it is the
  version most dangerous at a drake.
- **A completed first item on him while she has none** should end all consideration of contesting
  anything in melee range. His early lead is already the largest in this directory; an item on top of
  it is decisive.

## What his presence means for drakes and grubs

- **He takes objectives fast** — on-hit damage plus attack speed is exactly what an objective's health
  bar is weakest to — and he wins the contested fight around one.
- The early drake and grub window is **his** window. He is the counterpart most likely to be at a
  neutral objective before the timer and healthy when he gets there.
- The honest coaching line is that at even numbers she does not contest these early. She takes what he
  is not at, and she plays for the second half of the map rather than the first.

## HOW LILLIA PLAYS THE UDYR MATCHUP

**Who wins the early duel: Udyr, by the widest margin of any matchup in this directory.** Shield,
life steal, max-health on-hit damage, a repeatable stun with a dash attached. There is no level, no
health total and no bit of terrain before her first item at which this duel is worth taking. It is
not a coin flip she should decline; it is not a coin flip at all.

**Who wins the race: nobody outright, and that is the problem.** His clear is faster than hers early;
hers accelerates and catches up. Expect to be behind on CS for the first few minutes against a Udyr
and **do not chase that number with a fight** — chasing it is the single most likely way this matchup
kills her.

**When she may contest a crab or an objective:**

- When she can see him on the other side of the map and the objective can be taken and left before he
  crosses. Distance is the only real defence.
- When her team is present in numbers and she is the one arriving with the sleep after he commits.
- When he is visibly out of mana, which is the one state in which his kit genuinely stops working.

**When she absolutely may not contest:**

- Alone, at any health, in the first ten minutes. This is the flat rule of the matchup.
- Against an Awakened Stampede already coming at her. The sleep does not stop it — **the answer has
  to be leaving early, not answering late.**
- In his storm, where his slow and her movement speed cancel and the fight becomes stationary, which
  is the only kind of fight she loses outright.

**The trap specific to this matchup:** trying to keep up with him. He will be ahead on CS, he will be
at the early objectives, and the natural response is to contest something to close the gap. Every one
of those contests is a fight on his terms during the ten minutes he is strongest. **The correct
posture is to concede the early map, run uninterrupted routes, and let the game come to the part
where his lead stops growing** — and then spend her tempo on objectives with her team, never on a
rematch.
