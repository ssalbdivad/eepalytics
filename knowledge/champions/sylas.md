# Sylas — the counterpart, and how Lillia plays against him

Read when Sylas is the **enemy jungler**.

## What he is

A melee mage-bruiser with a heal that scales on how nearly dead he is, a two-part dash with a
knockup on the end, and an ultimate that takes someone else's. He is mana-hungry and his early
clear is unremarkable, but he wins the fight that goes long and he wins the fight that starts with
him at low health, which is the fight most junglers think they are winning.

## The kit, as it affects her

**Passive — Petricite Burst.** After each spell his next attack whirls his chains for bonus area
damage and he gains attack speed while the charge is held. His damage is therefore spell-and-attack
woven together, which is why his clear is better than his ability costs suggest and why standing
next to him for one extra second is worse than it looks.

**Q — Chain Lash.** A two-part ranged hit that slows, with **an explosion where the two chains
cross**. The explosion is most of the damage. It is his safest tool and the thing he pokes camps
and champions with from outside melee.

**W — Kingslayer.** A short lunge with damage, and **healing that increases the lower his health
is**, up to a large heal near death. This is the single most important fact about duelling Sylas:
**his health bar lies.** A Sylas at a sliver is one button from being back in the fight, and the
button is stronger precisely because he is low.

**E — Abscond / Abduct.** A dash, then a recast that throws his chains and **pulls him to the first
enemy hit, knocking them up**. So it is an escape and an engage in the same cooldown, and he
chooses which after he has seen what she does.

**R — Hijack.** He steals an enemy champion's ultimate and casts a copy of it with his own stats,
putting that champion's ultimate on a partial cooldown for him. It is a one-button answer to
whatever the enemy team's best ultimate happens to be.

**The interaction that matters here:** a hijacked **Lilting Lullaby** works for him. His own
abilities apply Lillia's Dream Dust marker — without the burn damage — which means he can set up
and land her sleep himself. A Lillia who assumes "he cannot use my ult because he cannot apply
dust" is wrong, and this is the kind of wrong that ends a fight. Assume that if she is in the game,
her sleep is available to him too.

I have not written his cooldowns, healing thresholds, knockup duration or the exact hijack cooldown
figures. Those move with patches.

## Reading his clear

- **Mana-hungry, and that is his one early weakness.** He wants blue and he feels its absence. A
  Sylas clearing without blue is on a shorter leash than his kit suggests.
- **His early clear is average, not fast.** Even CS against him is a Lillia slightly behind where
  she should be; ahead on CS against Sylas is the expected outcome of two uninterrupted routes.
- **A Sylas meaningfully behind on CS has been in lanes.** He is a good ganker with a knockup, so
  that farm went somewhere.
- **He scales.** Falling behind him on CS early is recoverable; letting him reach two items even in
  gold is not the same thing, because his items compound with a heal that already scales on danger.

## His ganks, and what makes them fail

He walks in and E-recasts into a knockup, or he opens with the Q explosion from range and then
closes. The knockup is reliable and it is the reason his ganks convert.

They fail on **mana** — a Sylas who spent his clear on Q has one button when he arrives — and on
**the recast**: the pull only triggers on the first enemy it hits, so a minion or a pet between him
and the target eats the engage. A lane with vision and any movement ability plays through him.

## What his items tell you

He builds ability power with durability attached, and the balance of the two is the tell.

- **Health and ability power together** means the bruiser plan: he intends to be in the fight for
  its whole duration, healing, and that is a fight she cannot out-last.
- **Burst-shaped ability power** means he wants a hijacked ultimate to delete someone, and the
  danger is concentrated in the seconds after he presses it.
- **A completed first item on him** is when the W heal stops being a top-up and starts being a
  reset. Before it, a duel with him can be finished. After it, mostly it cannot.

## What his presence means for drakes and grubs

- He is a competent pit fighter but not a dominant one — he has no objective-damage tools beyond
  ordinary damage, and no way to take one from range.
- **The danger at a pit is the hijack.** Whatever ultimate on her team the objective fight was
  going to be won with, he can be holding a copy of it. Fights around objectives against Sylas are
  decided by which ultimates he has had the chance to steal.
- His E gives him a fast entry into a pit and a fast exit from one, so "he is not here yet" holds
  for less time than the walk suggests.

## HOW LILLIA PLAYS THE SYLAS MATCHUP

**Who wins the early duel: Sylas, and by a wider margin the longer it lasts.** Lillia's plan is the
long kiting fight; his plan is the long fight where he heals more the closer he comes to dying.
Those plans collide and his wins, because her damage arrives over time and his healing is timed to
exactly that. The one point in her favour is that the knockup is his only hard CC and it is on a
cooldown she can watch.

**Who wins the race: Lillia, and it is the real win condition here.** His clear is slower and
mana-bound; hers accelerates and does not stop. Farm gaps against Sylas are gettable and they are
worth more than any skirmish she could win instead.

**When she may contest a crab or an objective:**

- When she has seen him use his E recently, so the pull and the escape are both down.
- When she is ahead in level, in open ground, with dust already on him and her ult ready — she
  wins the fight she opens, never the one he opens.
- When he is clearly low on mana, which for Sylas is a real and frequent state early.

**When she absolutely may not contest:**

- At even health after his first item. His heal makes even-health a lie.
- When he is **already low**. That is the moment his W is worth the most, and chasing a low Sylas
  is the most common way this matchup loses a game.
- In a corridor or against a wall, where the E pull is free and her speed is not an escape.
- While her own ult is on cooldown and his is not — including the case where his is hers.

**The trap specific to this matchup:** the finishing instinct. Against nearly every jungler a
health bar at a sliver means the fight is over. Against Sylas that sliver is the setup for his
strongest button. If he is low and the kill is not immediate, the correct move is to walk away and
spend the tempo on a camp or an objective, which he cannot heal back.
