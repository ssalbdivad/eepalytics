# Shaco — the counterpart, and how Lillia plays against him

Read when Shaco is the **enemy jungler**.

## What he is

An invisible early-game bully who fights with traps, from behind, at a moment he picked. He has the
strongest level-two and level-three invade in the game and the least reliable late game of anyone
in this directory. Everything he has is front-loaded, and everything he does depends on her not
knowing where he is.

## The kit, as it affects her

**Passive — Backstab.** His attacks deal extra damage **from behind**. He does not want to be in
front of anyone, ever, and his opening is always from the side or the back.

**Q — Deceive.** A short blink that makes him **invisible** for a few seconds, and his next attack
out of it hits hard and crits from behind. This is the entire champion: an approach she cannot see,
an escape she cannot follow, on a short cooldown. Using his boxes or his clone does not break the
invisibility.

**W — Jack In The Box.** A trap that arms, stealths itself, and **fears** whoever walks into it —
champions for a meaningful moment, monsters for longer. The boxes also deal **bonus damage to
monsters**, which is his clear and why his clear is fast without being healthy. Two consequences:

- **A fear is the worst crowd control that exists against Lillia.** Her escape is movement she
  controls; a fear takes the control away and walks her into him.
- **Boxes are placed in advance.** The fight happens where he already put them, which is usually
  the crab, the entrance to a camp, or the brush beside a pit.

**E — Two-Shiv Poison.** A ranged slow with an execute threshold — it deals bonus damage to targets
below a health cut-off. So his kill range is longer than his melee range, and a Lillia running away
at low health is inside it.

**R — Hallucinate.** He vanishes briefly and reappears with a **clone** that lasts several seconds,
explodes when it dies, and spawns small boxes that fear immediately. It is his escape, his burst,
and the reason a Shaco on screen is not necessarily the Shaco.

I have not written cooldowns, box arm or fear durations, invisibility duration or execute
thresholds.

## Reading his clear

- **Fast, and he pays for it in health.** Boxes clear camps quickly, but he takes damage doing it
  and he has no sustain in the kit. A Shaco who has just full-cleared is genuinely hurt, unlike
  Briar and unlike Viego.
- **A Shaco even on CS early has been farming, which is not his plan.** His plan is to be in her
  jungle before either of them has three levels.
- **A Shaco behind on CS with kills is exactly what the champion is for**, and he is the most
  likely jungler in this directory to be sitting invisible somewhere rather than standing on a
  camp.
- **He falls off.** A Shaco who did not get ahead in the first ten minutes is a champion with a
  blink and no teamfight. That is genuinely the plan: survive his first ten minutes.

## His ganks, and what makes them fail

He ganks from inside the lane brush he was already standing in, or with a blink from an angle the
lane was not watching, opening with a box fear. The fear is what converts them.

They fail on **vision that was placed before he arrived** — not on a ward placed when he shows,
because by then he is already invisible. They also fail when the box is killed: the boxes have low
health and they are visible while they arm, so a lane that hits one takes the whole gank away.

## What his items tell you

Shaco is the champion in this directory where the item feed changes what he *is*, so it is worth
reading carefully.

- **Lethality and attack-damage items** mean the ordinary assassin: he wants to be behind someone
  squishy with Deceive up, and his boxes are for clearing and for the fear.
- **Ability power items** mean the box build: his traps become the damage source, they hit far
  harder, and the danger is no longer where he is but **where he has been**. Against that Shaco, an
  unwarded pit entrance is lethal on its own, without him standing in it.
- **A completed first item on him** while she has none means the early skirmish window he was
  playing for is open, and it closes later than she would like.

## What his presence means for drakes and grubs

- He is a poor pit fighter in a straight contest, and a very good **thief**: a blink over the wall,
  an execute-flavoured smite window, and an escape that does not require walking out.
- **Boxes near the pit are placed before the objective is contested.** Walking into a drake fight
  against a Shaco means walking into whatever he set up during the two minutes before the timer.
- The vision that matters against him is not the pit but the two entrances and the brush, placed
  early rather than at the moment of the fight.

## HOW LILLIA PLAYS THE SHACO MATCHUP

**Who wins the early duel: Shaco, decisively, and he is choosing when it starts.** He opens
invisible from behind with a fear waiting. She has no burst to punish the moment he is visible and
her escape is a resource that a fear deletes. There is no health total early at which taking this
fight on his terms is correct.

**Who wins the race: Lillia, and it is almost her only lever.** He has no sustain and his clear
costs him health; hers accelerates and she can be running a full route while he is hunting. Every
minute he spends invisible waiting for her is a minute she spends taking camps — **but only if she
is not where he is waiting.**

**When she may contest a crab or an objective:**

- When she has warded or Swirlseeded the ground **before** arriving, not on arrival.
- When she is meaningfully ahead in level and health, in the open, with an exit that is not a
  corridor.
- Later in the game, when his window has closed and a Shaco with no lead is the weakest jungler on
  the map.

**When she absolutely may not contest:**

- In the first few levels, at a crab, without vision. The level-two and level-three invade is what
  he is built for and she is one of the worst junglers in the game at answering it.
- Anywhere she cannot see the ground she is standing on being empty. Against everyone else, "I do
  not know where he is" is a risk; against Shaco it is the assumption he is playing for.
- At low health anywhere, because of the ranged execute. Walking home hurt past a brush is how this
  matchup kills her.
- In her own jungle after he has been missing for a while. His jungle is not where he is.

**The trap specific to this matchup:** believing the map. Not seeing Shaco means nothing at all —
he is invisible on a short cooldown, his clone shows up where he is not, and his boxes fight
without him. The honest posture is to treat the early game as though he is always one screen away,
take the safe half of the map, and let the champion expire. **The Swirlseed rolled into the brush
ahead of her is the cheapest thing she owns and it is the answer to this entire matchup.**
