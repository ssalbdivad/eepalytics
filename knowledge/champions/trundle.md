# Trundle — read as the enemy jungler

Trundle is an **early-game duelling jungler**. He wants the fight the enemy jungler does not want
to take, and he wants it before anyone has scaled. Coach the matchup as a *tempo race he is
trying to turn into a brawl*, and the fix is almost always about denying him the brawl rather
than winning it.

**He is at his strongest in a straight 1v1, early.** Chomp is sustain and an attack-damage steal
on a 3.5s cooldown, so a duel that looks even on paper usually is not. A jungler who scales — and
most junglers scale better than he does — wins by refusing the even fight and taking the map
instead. Being "passive" into him early is generally correct play, not timidity.

**His pillar is terrain, and terrain is what he actually threatens.** Pillar of Ice is cast at
1000 range and cuts a corridor in half. That makes the places he is dangerous predictable: pits,
tri-brush, river chokes, jungle entrances — anywhere a wall across the middle turns a retreat into
a trap. Against a jungler whose escape is movement speed rather than a dash, that is the pairing
worth planning around.

**He contests objectives well.** Subjugate steals a percentage of a target's health, armour and
magic resist — so a drake or a herald started with him unaccounted for is a bigger gamble than it
is against most junglers. "Know where he is before you start it" is the executable version.

**He falls off relative to the field.** His case is early pressure converted into something
permanent. A game where nothing early goes his way is a game he has to win on someone else's
terms — which is the whole argument for farming through the phase where he is strongest.

## Live triggers

*Cooldowns are 16.17.1 tuning and will move. Ability roles and the terrain problem are durable.*

**When his roster level shows 6 and his own does not** → Subjugate exists (120s at rank 1) and
the duel that was bad is now much worse. The level is in the block; it is not on his screen.

**When his own roster level is 2 or more above Trundle's** → the window is open, and it is the
only kind of window that lasts against a champion who wants the even fight: he is not even.

**When a fight is happening beside a camp or a wave** → King's Tribute heals him whenever a unit
dies near him, so the same fight in open ground is a different fight. This is one of the few
things the *video* can genuinely support, because it is about where the fight is, not what
anybody's cooldowns are.

**When an objective timer is inside a minute and Trundle is alive** → the pit is where his pillar
and his ult are worth the most, and it is the worst place to be caught alone.

**When he is dead with real time on the clock** → this is the counter-jungle, and against Trundle
specifically it is the one time crossing the map is not a duel risk.

**Never say** "watch out for Trundle's pillar" with no trigger. It is true for the entire game and
tells him nothing about this minute.
